Screenshots are in `docs/screenshots/`. They show a local browser preview, not proof of GitHub Pages deployment. After publishing, the same interactions must work at the live URL. Final grading is controlled by the course; this checklist maps the supplied criteria to the implementation.

## Before submitting

1. Open the repository without being signed in and confirm its source is public.
2. Open the live site, then navigate to About Us and Contact Us.
3. Search `beach`, `temple`, and `Japan`; confirm two relevant images appear for each.
4. Search `Australia`; confirm Sydney and Melbourne appear.
5. Use Reset and confirm all eight recommendations return.
6. Try the contact form. Note that this static course demo validates inputs but does not send email.
7. Supply the actual public repository URL for Question 1. Supply the live Pages URL when the assignment asks for the website.
8. Attach the corresponding screenshots where requested.

The score is out of 20; the stated 70% passing threshold corresponds to 14 points.
